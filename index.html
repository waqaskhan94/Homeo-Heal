    Frequently asked questions

    How long is the first consultation?

    Usually 45 to 60 minutes. The doctor will ask about your main complaint and also about sleep, appetite, energy, emotions and past illnesses. Follow-up visits are shorter.

    Can I take homeopathy alongside my other medicines?

    Please tell us everything you currently take. Do not stop or change prescribed medicine without speaking to the doctor who prescribed it.

    Do you see children and older adults?

    Yes. Children are seen with a parent or guardian. Older patients are welcome to bring a family member for support.

    Can I have an online consultation?

    Yes. Choose “Online video consultation” when you book. We will send a link once the appointment is confirmed.

    What if I need to cancel or change my appointment?

    Message us on WhatsApp or call at least a day before and we’ll find you another time.

    Have another question? Request a call back and a team member will reach out.
