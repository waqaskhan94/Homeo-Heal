BHMS, Homeopathic Physician

How we work

Listening first, prescribing second

We believe good care starts with a good conversation. Your doctor will ask about your symptoms, sleep, appetite, stress and history, then explain the remedy they have chosen and why.

  • A clear history before any prescription
  • Plain-language explanations for you and your family
  • Honest referrals when you need tests or a specialist

Homeopathy is a complementary approach. It does not replace emergency care or specialist treatment.
